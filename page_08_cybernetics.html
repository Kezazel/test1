Sector 2 - Cyberpunk 2020 Specifications

Cyberpunk is a tabletop role-playing game in the dystopian science fiction genre, written by Mike Pondsmith and first published by R. Talsorian Games in 1988. It is typically referred to by its second or fourth edition names, Cyberpunk 2020 and Cyberpunk Red, in order to distinguish it from the cyberpunk genre after which it is named.

Neural jacks and optical interfaces line the testing consoles. Engineers calibrated telemetry links to the flight hangar, whispering that the fastest route is to proceed directly to Aerospace Division.

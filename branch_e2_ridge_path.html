A phantom island is a purported island which has appeared on maps but was later found to not exist. They usually originate from the reports of early sailors exploring new regions, and are commonly the result of navigational errors, mistaken observations, unverified misinformation, or deliberate fabrication. Some of these islands in high latitudes may also result from iceberg sightings. Some have remained on maps for centuries before being "un-discovered".
